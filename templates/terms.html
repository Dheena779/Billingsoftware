1. Description of Cloud Services

Billora provides multi-tenant SaaS (Software as a Service) solutions for retailers, supermarkets, pharmacies, apparel stores, and eateries. Core platform features include:

  • Batch-wise stock inventory tracking with First-In First-Out (FIFO) cost management.
