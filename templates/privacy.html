Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 | Effective Date: October 2026

At Billora (founded and operated by Dheena, headquartered in Vellore, Tamil Nadu, India), we are committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of store owners, cashiers, and your store customers. This Privacy Policy outlines our data handling practices for our cloud billing and retail management platform.

1. Information We Collect

  • Store Account Information: Business Name, Owner Name, Store Code, City, State, and Google / Gmail Address used for account authentication and verification.
  • Store Operations Data: Products, barcodes, stock quantities, purchase costs, retail selling prices, invoices, customer purchase histories, and staff attendance logs.
  • Payment & Transaction Identifiers: Razorpay Payment IDs and Order IDs associated with your license activation. We never store or have access to your bank accounts, debit/credit card CVVs, or UPI PINs.

2. Strict Multi-Tenant Data Isolation

Billora is engineered with strict multi-tenant architecture. Every database query, sales report, product inventory, and bill receipt is partitioned exclusively by your unique store_id. Under no circumstances can any other store access or view your sales revenue, customer data, or product pricing.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your business information is strictly used to:

  • Maintain, operate, and optimize your cloud billing terminals and reports.
  • Send email verification links to authenticate and secure your store account.
  • Issue single-use license keys upon successful Razorpay subscription payments.
  • Provide direct founder customer support and resolve technical queries.

4. Zero Data Selling & Third-Party Policy

We never sell, rent, or trade your business, sales, or customer data to marketing agencies, ad brokers, or third parties. Your data belongs solely to your store.

5. Data Security & Retention

All passwords are protected using industry-standard one-way cryptographic hashing (PBKDF2/SHA-256). All communications between your devices and Billora servers are secured using SSL/TLS encryption. Store data is retained safely on persistent storage and will only be deleted upon your explicit request.
