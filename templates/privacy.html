Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Cloud Billing Software (operated by Dheena, Vellore, Tamil Nadu) collects, protects, and uses the information you provide when using our SaaS platform.

1. Information We Collect

  • Store Account Data: Business Name, Owner Name, Contact Mobile Number, Email Address, City, and State.
  • Operational Store Data: Products, barcodes, stock quantities, invoices, customer purchase records, and staff attendance records.
  • Transaction Records: Payment IDs and order identifiers processed securely via Razorpay (we do NOT store credit/debit card numbers or UPI PINs on our servers).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to:

  • Provide and operate your cloud billing terminal and dashboard.
  • Process license activation keys and subscription renewals.
  • Provide customer support and technical troubleshooting.

3. Data Protection & Multi-Tenant Isolation

We implement multi-tenant database isolation. Each store's records are strictly partitioned by store ID, preventing cross-store data leakage.
