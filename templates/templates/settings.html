♻️ Restore from a backup

Replace everything in this software with the contents of a backup file.

This replaces all current data - products, bills, settings and logins. A safety copy of the current data is saved first in data/backups, so you can undo a wrong restore. You will be logged out afterwards and must use the username and password from the backup.

💰 GST / Tax

Show a GST breakdown on invoices.

Your product prices are treated as GST-inclusive: the bill total does not change. The invoice shows the taxable value and the CGST + SGST contained in the total. One rate applies to the whole bill. Bills made while GST is on keep their rate, and bills made before are not changed.
Common rates: 0, 5, 12, 18, 28.
