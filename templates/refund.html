Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 | Effective Date: October 2026

This Cancellation and Refund Policy applies to all digital cloud subscriptions, Pro access plans, and license activations purchased on Billora (Founder & Owner: Dheena, Headquartered in Vellore, Tamil Nadu, India).

✨ Risk-Free 7-Day Free Trial: We offer every new business owner an unrestricted 7-day free trial so you can test all POS billing features, barcode scanning, thermal receipt printing, and inventory tracking without entering any payment details.

1. Subscription Model & Non-Recurring Policy

Billora operates with complete payment transparency. We do not charge hidden fees and we do not use recurring auto-debit traps.

  • You only pay when you choose to renew your store's Pro subscription (Monthly, Quarterly, Half-Yearly, or Annual).
  • If your subscription period concludes without renewal, your store data remains 100% safe and intact in read-only mode until you choose to reactivate.

2. Refund Eligibility Criteria

We take pride in our software quality. You are entitled to a full 100% refund under the following conditions:

  • Service Unavailability / Downtime: If our cloud platform experiences major server disruptions preventing billing and our technical team cannot resolve it within 48 hours.
  • License Key Activation Failure: If an online payment is debited from your account via Razorpay but the single-use license key fails to activate your store subscription.
  • Double Billing / Duplicate Payment: If multiple charges occur for a single order due to network glitches during payment processing.

3. Non-Refundable Scenarios

  • Refund requests submitted after 7 calendar days from the transaction date.
  • Failure of third-party hardware owned by the user (such as incompatible local printers, faulty barcode scanners, or local internet outage at the store).
  • Change of mind after extensive operational usage during an active paid period.
