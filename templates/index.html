Powerful Features

Everything Your Store Needs to Grow

Engineered for superfast checkout speeds, accurate multi-batch stock tracking, and complete financial peace of mind.

⚡

5-Second POS Billing

Instant item search, laser barcode scanner integration, keyboard shortcuts, and automated discount calculation. Zero lag during busy rush hours.

🖨️

Thermal & A4 Printing

Direct printing on standard 58mm & 80mm thermal receipt printers, or full A4/A5 invoices with your custom store logo and GST details.

📦

Smart Cloud Inventory

Stock automatically updates with every sale. Track purchase batches, cost price margins, expiry dates, and receive low-stock alerts before items run out.

📊

Reports & Profit Analysis

Comprehensive daily sales summaries, net profit analytics, best-selling product charts, and downloadable GST summaries for filing.

👥

Staff Management & Roles

Separate cashier accounts from owner admin panel. Cashiers only bill; owners see all revenue, profits, and staff attendance punch-ins.

📱

Multi-Device Cloud Access

Access your store anywhere. Check today’s live sales from your mobile phone while your cashier handles customers on the billing computer.
